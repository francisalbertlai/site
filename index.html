# Albert Lai Melbourne. Deceased estates by day, fusion by night. --- ## Threads **Conversion.** Most human activity reduces to converting energy, money, and time into each other — and most friction lives at the badly-designed conversion points. Fusion is the largest unclaimed one. **Translation.** Hard ideas rarely fail because they're hard. They fail at the door — the particular way a particular mind needs to receive them. Years of tutoring suggest the door matters more than the idea. What this skill is ultimately for: open question. **Building before understanding.** A startup built from zero coding knowledge made $200 total and taught more than a degree did. Working theory since then: build the thing first, let the building decide the qualifications. Results mixed, method retained. **Perception vs. reality.** Most conflicts, examined closely, turn out to be with the map, not the territory. Physics is a good teacher here — it doesn't negotiate, which turns out to be oddly comforting rather than bleak. **Mess.** Everything real is chaotic and embarrassing in the middle. This appears to be a feature. **Death.** Working near it daily turns out to be a very effective filter for what actually matters. Fewer things than you'd think.
